Apps

Install once. Stay up to date.

Each app comes from its own GitHub releases, and nothing is installed until it checks out against the release key built into the launcher. A new version is unpacked next to the old one and swapped in; if anything fails, the old copy stays.

Signed

Verified before anything is replaced

Every download is checked against the app's signature (minisign or Ed25519) before it touches your apps. A file that doesn't match is thrown away.

Careful

Your work comes first

An open app is never replaced or removed. Removing an app keeps its documents, settings and data, and copies you built from source are shown, never touched.

On Linux

Where apps belong

~/.local/share/lsuite/apps, with a menu entry for each app (macOS and Windows are coming soon). The launcher updates itself the same way.

lsuite account

Sign in once. Every app is signed in.

The launcher holds the same lsuite account every app reads: sign in through your browser or with a key, and with lsuite Pass the agents in all four apps work through lsuite AI. It shows your plan, the month's allowance and your cloud storage; plans are chosen on lsuite.xyz.

A free account is all the apps need. They stay free; lsuite Pass is the one optional thing lsuite sells. A demo for now: no payment is taken.

lsuite Cloud

Your files, on every computer.

lsuite Cloud comes with lsuite Pass, and the launcher is where you manage it. Your documents stay plain files on your disk; nothing goes to the cloud unless you put it there.

Files

Upload and download

Upload files or whole folders, or drop them on the window. Downloads are checked against their SHA-256.

Folders

Organise

New folder, rename, move and delete, with a confirmation before anything goes. Usage against your plan, always in view.

Sync

A folder kept in step

Pair a folder on your computer with one in the cloud and it syncs both ways. When both sides changed, both versions are kept.

Plus comes with 50 GB, Pro with 250 GB, Studio with 1 TB. See the plans.

Agent and scripts

Your agent can set up the suite.

Every action is a command, the same from the window, lsuite-cli and lsuite-mcp: install an app, update them all, upload a folder to the cloud.

You decide what an agent may do. Installing and cloud uploads are on; removing apps, deleting cloud files and signing in or out stay off until you turn them on in Settings › Agents.

# install or update from a script
lsuite-cli apps.install app=kimchi
lsuite-cli apps.updateAll

# or hand it to Claude Code
claude mcp add lsuite -- /path/to/lsuite-mcp

Questions

Before you install.

Do I need the launcher to get the apps?

Yes: the ready-made builds of the four apps come through it. Install the ones you want, update them all at once, and hold the one account they share. Their source stays open (MIT), so you can always build an app yourself.

Do I need an account?

A free one, to get the apps and their updates: no plan, no payment. lsuite Pass (lsuite AI, lsuite Cloud and the lsuite Marketplace) is optional.

How do I know a download is genuine?

The launcher carries each app's release key and checks every file's signature before it installs. If a signature doesn't match, nothing is replaced. Its own updates are signed the same way.

What does lsuite Cloud cost?

It comes with lsuite Pass: 50 GB with Plus, 250 GB with Pro, 1 TB with Studio. On Free you can still list, download and delete files already there. A demo for now: no payment is taken and storage is capped small.

Will it touch the copies I built myself?

No. Copies outside the usual places, such as builds from source, are listed and left alone.

Does it send anything about me?

No telemetry. It asks lsuite.xyz for the apps and their builds, your account and your cloud files, and GitHub for its own updates. Nothing else.
