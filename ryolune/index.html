Finish the song.
Hand the busywork to an agent.

ryolune is a complete DAW in beta: record while you hear yourself through your effects, arrange, edit MIDI, mix on a real mixer and export stems, with 35 stock instruments and effects and your own CLAP, VST3 and Audio Units. When you'd rather not quantise forty notes by hand, ask the built-in agent, or plug Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client straight into the song.

New in 0.14

Black and white, and at home with your old songs.

ryolune takes on lsuite's new look: black and white, square, with film grain behind the chrome. It opens songs from other music apps, keeps the agent's conversations with each song, lets you steer the agent while it works, and tells you what changed after an update.

The look

Ink on paper

Everything is a grey and what you choose is inverted; red only means recording. Every area has a title bar, tools are boxed by kind, and track headers keep mute, solo and arm in view.

Coming from another app

DAWproject in and out

Tracks, sends, MIDI, audio with fades, markers, tempo, automation and plugins with their settings, from Bitwig Studio, Studio One, Cubase and others, with a report of what came across.

First run

Set up in a minute

Which app you made music in, whether you want AI, which agent, and a sound check; then the demo song, an empty song or your old one.

The agent

Conversations, kept

Each song keeps its conversations and a project memory the agent reads first: the key, the style, what to leave alone.

The agent

Steer while it works

Type while it runs: Send becomes Steer. The agent reads your note at its next step and keeps what it has done.

Updates

What's new, and what went wrong

A sheet of what changed after each update, logs and crash reports in Settings › Diagnostics, and Report a Problem. Nothing leaves your computer.

Every version · ryolune 0.14 release notes

Features

Everything between an idea and a bounced master.

Record and arrange

Hear yourself, then shape the song

Arm a track and hear yourself through its effects, with a count-in. Name sections with markers and loop them; split, trim, fade and snap from bars to 1/64.

MIDI

Three editors on the same notes

Piano roll, score and step views, with controller lanes. Quantise, humanise, legato, velocity ramps, fit to scale and transpose.

Mixing

A real mixer

Fader, pan, mute, solo, arm and live meters per channel, eight inserts a strip, buses and sends, a master chain and automation of any parameter.

Tempo

Songs that breathe

Tempo changes and ramps on a tempo track. Notes and markers stay on their bars; exports and the metronome follow.

Sound from words

Describe a sound

Loops and one-shots from ElevenLabs, Stable Audio, fal or your own endpoint, and Sample Keys to play them.

Export

Mixes and stems

WAV up to 96 kHz or 32-bit float, AIFF, FLAC and Ogg Vorbis, for the song, a range or one file per track, plus MIDI and DAWproject.

Plugins

35 in the box. Yours too. Or ask for one.

Twelve instruments, from a polysynth and an electric piano to an 808 and a string ensemble, and twenty-three effects, each drawing what it does from the same maths as the audio. Your own plugins are scanned in a separate process, so one bad plugin can't take the app down.

Build with your agent · next release

Describe the plugin. Your agent writes it in Rust.

ryolune 0.15 adds a Plugins window with one field: "Describe the plugin you want". The agent scaffolds a crate from the SDK, builds it until it's green, installs it and plays it, without a restart. It offers to install Rust if you don't have it, and it only builds plugins when you've allowed it to.

# the recipe the agent follows (PLUGINS.md)
ryolune-cli plugin.guide
ryolune-cli plugin.new name=tape-wobble kind=effect
ryolune-cli plugin.build name=tape-wobble
ryolune-cli plugin.publishLocal name=tape-wobble

Agent

Ask for the edit. Keep the undo.

The agent panel shows one card per command it runs. It uses the model you already have: Codex or Claude Code, an Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, DeepSeek or xAI key, or Ollama or LM Studio on this computer.

  • Undo from here. The Changes tab lists every change the agent made; roll back to any point.
  • On a leash you hold. Permissions gate files, transport, replacing the song, settings and quitting, for the panel and MCP clients alike.
  • Bring your own agent. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, Zed and any MCP app get a ready connection to the open song.
  • Nothing leaves your machine until you connect a provider and send a prompt.
# the whole song in one call
ryolune-cli session.overview
ryolune-cli track.add --kind midi --name Keys --instrument "E-Piano Mk I"
ryolune-cli strip.setPlugin --trackId master --slot 7 --pluginId stock:Limiter
ryolune-cli history.undo
ryolune-cli --file song.ryolune session.exportAudio --path mix.flac

# plug it into Claude Code, live on the open song
claude mcp add ryolune -- "$(jq -r .mcp ~/.lsuite/apps/ryolune.json)" --live

The window, the agent, ryolune-cli and ryolune-mcp run the same 239 commands, with the same undo history.
