Signature colors

Each app owns one hue.

Same lightness and chroma for all three (OKLCH), only the hue changes, so they read as one family. The accent is step 400 in dark mode and 600 in light. Violet, green, amber and pink are kept for the next apps.

Anatomy of a window

Glass around the work, never on it.

Sidebars, toolbars, inspectors, transport and popovers are glass over the window's tinted backdrop. The canvas, timeline or document stays solid, so what you make is never blurred or tinted.

Glass tiers

Three levels of frost.

The higher a surface floats, the more opaque it gets, so text stays readable over anything. With "reduce transparency" turned on, every tier becomes solid.

Rules

What every app follows.

Accent means "yours or active". Selection, focus, the playhead, the primary button and what the agent touched. Nothing else is colored with it.

States keep their own colors. Record and errors are red, mute and solo keep theirs, meters keep green to amber. The accent never replaces a meaning.

Glass only on chrome. Work surfaces (timeline, canvas, editor, tables of data) are solid bg-raised.

Contrast is tested. Text on every glass tier passes 4.5:1 over the worst backdrop, in both modes. Fix the tier's opacity, never the threshold.

Native vibrancy where it exists. macOS uses the window material (Tauri window-vibrancy, AppKit); Windows uses Mica; elsewhere the CSS tiers.

Motion is quick. 120, 200 or 320 ms with one ease; drags and playback are never eased; everything stops under reduced motion.
