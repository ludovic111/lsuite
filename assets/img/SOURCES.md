# Images

App icons and screenshots were generated from the native lsuite app repositories on 2026-10-07.
The light and dark captures show actual running apps, with demonstration documents.

The photo visible in `nori/editor.webp` and `nori/editor-light.webp` is
[Red Acer by mendhak](https://www.flickr.com/photos/mendhak/54872031489/), licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The author also states the
license in the [Ubuntu wallpaper submission](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/t/resolute-raccoon-26-04-wallpaper-competition/73131/11).
It has been cropped and combined with vector and text layers in nori. These adapted image
captures are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0; this attribution also applies to their use on the home page.

Third-party app/provider logo sources remain documented in `logos/SOURCES.md`.
