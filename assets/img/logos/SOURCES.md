These logos are the trademarks of their owners. lsuite shows them only to name the services and
apps its apps work with, next to their names; it isn't endorsed by or affiliated with them. Their
colours are kept; a mark drawn in black gets a `-dark` copy in white for dark pages (picked with
`prefers-color-scheme`). Simple Icons artwork is CC0.
