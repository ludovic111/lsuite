What it does

Three tools, one document.

Raster layers, vector shapes and pages live side by side in one .nori file: plain data in Rust, drawn on the GPU in lsuite's black-and-white look. All three share one canvas and one undo history.

Photo

Layers and masks

Pixel and adjustment layers, masks and blend modes. Embedded smart objects preserve their originals when resized, with separate document tabs and undo histories.

Vector

Paths, shapes and type

Pen paths, shapes, strokes and fills that stay sharp at any size, next to the pixels they sit on.

Layout

Pages and text frames

Pages, margins and grids, text that flows from frame to frame, and images placed from the same document.

Adjust

Adjustments and filters

Levels, an interactive RGB/channel curves graph, hue and saturation, .cube LUTs, blurs and sharpening, worked out in tiles at full precision.

Paint

A brush engine

Size, flow, spacing, hardness and pressure controls, an eraser, and brushes from .gbr files; swatches from .ase.

Files

Open formats

PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, BMP, layered PSD and OpenRaster export; PDF with selectable text; IDML in and out; PDF-compatible Illustrator and 8-bit GIMP XCF import; and the .nori document, a file you can read.

Coming from another editor

Three apps you'd pay for, in one you don't.

What you'd do in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign happens in one nori document. It opens Photoshop documents with their layers and imports GIMP brushes, so your work comes along. Complex PSD and IDML content uses rendered fallbacks; Illustrator import needs PDF compatibility. Format and tablet support details.

Agent and scripts

Your AI can design too.

One command registry for the window, the built-in agent, nori-cli and nori-mcp. The agent uses lsuite AI or the model you already have: Claude Code, Codex, an API key or a local model. It shows one card per command, and you decide what it may touch.

# Claude Code drives the open image
claude mcp add nori -- /Applications/nori.app/Contents/MacOS/nori-mcp --live

# or a script, on the app or a .nori file
nori-cli doc.overview
nori-cli --file poster.nori export.file path=poster.png
Plugins

Describe a filter. Your agent writes it in Rust.

nori's filters are plugins: the stock ones ship with it, and the nori-plugin SDK runs yours on the same tiles, at full precision, without blocking the canvas. In the Plugins area, "Describe the plugin you want" starts your agent on it: it writes the crate, builds it until it's green and loads it without a restart.

Stock

The filters and adjustments nori ships with, listed with what they do.

Installed

Yours, switchable

Every plugin on the computer with its version and an on/off switch always in view. A failing filter is switched off and its edit is discarded.
