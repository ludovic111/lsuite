nori is lsuite's image and design app, joining the suite in beta: retouch a photo, draw a logo and lay out the poster it goes on, in the same document, the work of Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign in one app. A Rust engine draws it all; like every lsuite app, everything you can click is a command, so you, a script or an AI agent can make the same edits, with one undo.

What it does

Three tools, one document.

Raster layers, vector shapes and pages live side by side in one .nori file: plain data in Rust, drawn on the GPU in lsuite's black-and-white look. This is what nori 0.1 is being built to do.

Photo

Layers and masks

Pixel and adjustment layers with masks, blend modes and opacity, grouped and reordered like you expect.

Vector

Paths, shapes and type

Pen paths, shapes, strokes and fills that stay sharp at any size, next to the pixels they sit on.

Layout

Pages and text frames

Pages, margins and grids, text that flows from frame to frame, and images placed from the same document.

Adjust

Adjustments and filters

Levels, curves, hue and saturation, colour balance, .cube LUTs, blurs and sharpening, worked out in tiles at full precision.

Paint

A brush engine

Pressure, spacing and hardness, an eraser and a smudge, and brushes from .gbr files; swatches from .ase.

Files

Open formats

PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, BMP and GIF in and out, PSD and OpenRaster layers in, and the .nori document, a file you can read.

Coming from another editor

Three apps you'd pay for, in one you don't.

What you'd do in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign happens in one nori document. It's being built to open Photoshop documents with their layers, and GIMP brushes, so your work comes along.

Agent and scripts

Your AI can design too.

One command registry for the window, the built-in agent, nori-cli and nori-mcp. The agent uses lsuite AI or the model you already have: Claude Code, Codex, an API key or a local model. It shows one card per command, and you decide what it may touch.

# Claude Code drives the open image
claude mcp add nori -- /Applications/nori.app/Contents/MacOS/nori-mcp --live

# or a script, on the app or a .nori file
nori-cli document.overview
nori-cli --file poster.nori export.png path=poster.png
Plugins

Describe a filter. Your agent writes it in Rust.

nori's filters are plugins: the stock ones ship with it, and the nori-plugin SDK runs yours on the same tiles, at full precision, without blocking the canvas. In the Plugins area, "Describe the plugin you want" starts your agent on it: it writes the crate, builds it until it's green and loads it without a restart.

Stock

In the box

The filters and adjustments nori ships with, listed with what they do.

Installed

Yours, switchable

Every plugin on the computer with its version and an on/off switch always in view. A plugin that crashes is turned off, not the app.
