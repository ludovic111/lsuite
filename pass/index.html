Under the hood

It speaks Anthropic's API.

The apps reach lsuite AI with the Anthropic provider they already have, pointed at lsuite. Claude Code runs through it too, with a key from your account.

Requests go through lsuite's server to Anthropic and back; lsuite keeps the token counts it needs for your allowance, not what you asked. The apps send nothing else: no telemetry.

# Claude Code on your lsuite Pass
ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=%ORIGIN%/api/ai \
ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN=lsk_… claude

# an app's CLI, with the key from your account
kimchi-cli account.signIn key=lsk_…
kimchi-cli account.status
Questions

Before you sign in.

Is this a real subscription?

Not yet. The demo takes no payment, lsuite AI returns sample responses, and cloud storage is capped small. Yes. Subscribe through Stripe; renewals are monthly in USD. Manage or cancel lsuite Pass from your account. Bringing your own provider stays free.

Do I need it to use the apps?

No. Every app is free and open source with every feature, and works with the model you already have: Claude Code, Codex, an API key or a local model. Your files stay on your disk. lsuite Pass is for people who'd rather not set any of that up, want their files on every computer, or want plugins others made.

What happens when my allowance runs out?

The agent stops and says so in one line, with Manage plan. It never moves you to another provider without asking. The allowance comes back on the first of the month. Cloud and the marketplace keep working.

Can I publish plugins without a Pass?

Yes. Anyone with an lsuite account can publish to the marketplace; installing from it comes with a paid plan. Creators aren't paid yet: a revenue share is for when payments are live.

Which apps does one account cover?

All of them, and the lsuite launcher. Signing in from any lsuite app signs in every lsuite app on the computer; signing out from one signs that copy out.

How do I sign in without a browser?

Your account page makes a key (lsk_…) to paste into an app's CLI or a headless machine. A new key replaces the last one.
