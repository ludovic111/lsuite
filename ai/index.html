Under the hood

It speaks Anthropic's API.

The apps reach lsuite AI with the Anthropic provider they already have, pointed at lsuite. Claude Code runs through it too, with a key from your account.

Requests go through lsuite's server to Anthropic and back; lsuite keeps the token counts it needs for your allowance, not what you asked. The apps send nothing else: no telemetry.

# Claude Code on your lsuite AI plan
ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL=%ORIGIN%/api/ai \
ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN=lsk_… claude

# an app's CLI, with the key from your account
kimchi-cli account.signIn key=lsk_…
kimchi-cli account.status
Questions

Before you sign in.

Is this a real subscription?

Not yet. lsuite AI is a demo: checkout takes no payment and starts the plan at once, and until it goes live the agents answer with a short message saying so. Your account, plan, allowance and the sign-in from the apps all work, so the apps can be built and tried against it.

Do I need it to use the apps?

No. Every app is free and open source with every feature, and works with the model you already have: Claude Code, Codex, an API key or a local model. lsuite AI is for people who'd rather not set any of that up.

What happens when my allowance runs out?

The agent stops and says so in one line, with Manage plan. It never moves you to another provider without asking. The allowance comes back on the first of the month.

Which apps does one account cover?

All of them. Signing in from any lsuite app signs in every lsuite app on the computer; signing out from one signs that copy out.

How do I sign in without a browser?

Your account page makes a key (lsk_…) to paste into an app's CLI or a headless machine. A new key replaces the last one.
