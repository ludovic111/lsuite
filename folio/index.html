What it does

Three office apps, one window.

A report, the sheet behind its numbers and the slides that present them sit side by side in one app, as files on your disk. A live link keeps the report and slides in step when the numbers change.

Documents

Write

Headings, styles, lists, tables, images and footnotes, laid out in pages as you type.

Spreadsheets

Calculate

Cells, formulas and functions, several sheets per file, sorting, filters and charts from your numbers.

Presentations

Present

Slides with layouts, text, images and charts, a presenter view and full-screen playback.

Agent and scripts

Your AI can do the paperwork.

One command registry for the window, the built-in agent, folio-cli and folio-mcp. Ask for a summary of a report, a sheet filled from a CSV or a deck from your notes; the agent uses the model you already have, shows one card per command, and you decide what it may touch.

# Claude Code drives the open file
claude mcp add folio -- "$(jq -r .mcp ~/.lsuite/apps/folio.json)" --live
Plugins

Need a function it doesn't have? Ask for it.

folio has plugins like every lsuite app: the stock ones ship with it, and in the Plugins area, "Describe the plugin you want" starts your agent writing one in Rust. It builds it until it's green and loads it without a restart; building plugins is a permission you give it.
